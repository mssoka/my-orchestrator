  - **The void:** a Principled-Volume "fog" cube with its default opaque
    Principled BSDF surface renders as a grey WALL. Volume materials need
    Transparent BSDF surface + Volume output.

    center∓2.1). The window slab sat INSIDE its wall (dark disc on the
    render). Compute face positions from center±scale; keep emitters 0.02
    proud.
