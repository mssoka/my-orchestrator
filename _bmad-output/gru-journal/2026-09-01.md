## Lessons learned (the asset-pipeline era — hard-won, in order of cost)

1. **Downloads are session-state; `open_mainfile` discards unsaved imports.**
   The Muwanga desk and the Sanyo recorder each vanished TWICE this way.
   Iron rule: save IMMEDIATELY after any Sketchfab/Polyhaven download (or
   user Ctrl+S) — and never `open_mainfile` between a download and its save.
2. **Bridge saves are unreliable; the user's GUI Ctrl+S is ground truth.**
   The addon's texture *materials* never persist at all — download texture
   FILES direct (dl.polyhaven.org) and build PBR materials by hand.
3. **Collection instances draw contents at their LIBRARY world transforms.**
   The museum grid scattered every asset across scenes (floating envelopes,
   the recorder 60m away). Every collection must be origin-normalized
   (bbox center → 0, base z → 0) BEFORE the library saves.
4. **Blender 5.2 uniquifier crash:** '-' or '.' in ID names → SIGABRT in
   BLI_string_split_name_number (stoi). Sanitize names to [A-Za-z0-9_].
5. **Static posed sculpts are terrible character donors** (the muntashir
   war-veteran saga): fused props → amputation holes; bone-heat fails on
   fragmented scan meshes (numpy distance-field skinning is the fallback);
   heuristic island surgery never converges. CAST RIGGED T-POSE CHARACTERS
   FROM THE START — the jungle_jim businessman (66-bone rig, fingers) poses
   cleanly out of the box.
6. **GLTF pose craft:** pose rotations are LOCAL-axis — the bone's long axis
   (local Y) is twist-only, swings need local X. Print the bone list FIRST
   (LeftUpLeg_60, not guesses). Poses can silently fail to persist through
   save/reload — re-apply + verify values before rendering. Leg-bend SIGN
   varies per rig — verify with a side-view render.
7. **Import sizes lie.** Sketchfab's target_size normalize can be 2.4× off;
   WoodenTable_02 is a 0.42m side table. Always measure the world bbox after
   import and scale/center by measurement.
8. **Staging grammar:** judge BEHIND the desk facing camera; a chair-back
   toward camera is a lens-blocking slab (two renders lost).
9. **Raycasts don't hit collection-instance geometry** — measure the linked
   mesh directly (deck analysis for the bookshelf).
10. **C-grade = post process, proven on a real frame** (crushed B&W + spot
    reds via PIL; production version lives in the compositor with masks).
11. **Bridge timeouts ≠ failure** — long Cycles renders finish server-side;
    check the output file before assuming.
12. **Sketchfab/Polyhaven flags + keys are PER-SCENE** (`blendermcp_use_*`
    scene props) — copy them into new scenes or searches die with
    "Unknown command type".
13. **Downscale review renders BEFORE reading them into context** — full
    1920×1080 PNGs 413 the harness and bloat context to compaction
    (960×540 review_small.png is plenty for staging verdicts).
14. **GLTF-import hierarchies can ignore root scaling** (parent-inverse
    chains) — `bpy.ops.transform.resize` with center_override + constraint
    axis on the selected family WORKS where `root.scale` no-ops.
15. **A collection organized IN a scene renders TWICE** — once directly
    (its member meshes at their own transforms) and once per instance.
    The "ghost tree glued to the rover" (5 renders of moving every
    instance, rays naming the source meshes) was the source collection
    sitting in the scene. FIX: `layer_collection.children['A_x'].exclude
    = True` right after organizing — instancing keeps working, the
    direct render dies. (Same ghost as the floating-envelope era.)
16. **Nuclear-normalize for cursed imports**: unparent every mesh with
    matrix_world preserved, THEN bbox-shift each object directly — the
    only reliable cure when parent-inverses fight (the Range Rover took
    three passes; it also explained the "roof-floating" car).
17. **A library slot can silently hold the WRONG asset** — A_sedan was
    my hand-built box all along; the real Chrysler had been eaten to 2
    orphan meshes. Verify a slot's materials/contents before staging
    from it. (The Dacia Logan 2008 that replaced it is closer to canon
    anyway: "gunmetal-grey late-2000s compact sedan".)
18. **IMPACT AUDIT after every move (user ruling)** — anything moved
    might collide with/cover other items in the space (the cabinet flip
    covered the degree; the degree shift landed on the clock). After ANY
    placement change, run a pairwise bbox overlap check against neighbors
    BEFORE rendering/committing. Same for orientation: verify the FRONT
    faces the way the camera/room reads (two cabinets showed their backs).
    Clean-transform builds (location carries position, no baked offsets)
    make audits possible — baked-offset objects lie to the auditor.
19. **ASSETS-FIRST DOCTRINE (user ruling, standing)** — search Sketchfab/
    Polyhaven for an existing asset BEFORE building anything, up to and
    including FULL FURNISHED ROOMS (office, hallway, kitchen — download
    and modify). Hand-build only when the hunt comes back empty. Every
    hand-built piece is a failure of search, not a shortcut. And every
    downloaded asset banks into the library for future tracks.
