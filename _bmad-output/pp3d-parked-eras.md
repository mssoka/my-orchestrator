Principle (the Mario Galaxy rule): **every level stays a planet — only the
costume changes.** The sphere, the connect verb, the packets, the rocketship
meta, and the low-poly little-planet look are constants. Scope rule:
**one additive system per era — new verb never.**

The three-tier delivery model is constant in every era (this is how the real
internet is built — access → aggregation → backbone):
- **Last mile** — buildings → local node. The E1 verb. NEVER changes.
- **Aggregation** — town / island / city PoP. NEVER changes.
- **Long haul** — PoP → PoP across the world. **The only tier that wears the
  era costume.** The era answers one question: what does the long journey
  between towns look like this decade?

## PARKED eras

- **ERA-2 · ISP boom (~1995) — the ocean planet.** Ocean world; islands are
  the land, each island a little E1 (last mile unchanged). Long haul:
  submarine cables — draw the route, the cable ship lays it; landing stations
  are the beachheads; upgrade = lay a second cable (TAT-8/TAT-9 pattern);
  repeaters on long runs. Hazards: trawlers, anchor drags, shark bites →
  cable cut → reroute while a repair ship sails (all real events).
  +1 system: water rendering + cable ships.
- **ERA-3 · Backbone era — the ring planet.** The planet grows a ring; the
  surface keeps its cities. Long haul: uplink towers shoot packets to the
  orbital ring, which comes down half a world away (Token-Ring nostalgia is
  era-correct). Datacenter moons = first taste of DCs as destinations.
  +1 system: orbital layer.
- **ERA-4 · Cloud era — the machine world.** The whole planet becomes one
  friendly datacenter. Long haul: the machine world's interior fiber. The
  new delight is a zoom layer: dive into a DC district → ant-farm cutaway,
  packets in pipes. Hazards: outage cascades, thermal events (the cloud's
  own weather). +1 system: interior zoom.
- **ERA-5 · Satellite era — the constellation shell.** Birds in inclined
  orbits around the planet; links hop satellite → satellite; ground uplinks
  replace landing stations/towers. Hazards: rain fade, handoffs, and the
  Kessler cascade — a debris event cascades through the shell, reroute
  around dying orbits. Dovetails with the constellation view already
  planned. +1 system: satellite shell + debris events.
- **NIGHT · every era — Earth-at-Night rule (brand glue).** Connectivity =
  light: delivered internet glows on the night side; unconnected places stay
  dark. Board health readable from orbit at a glance; the win state in every
  era is the same sentence: *you lit up the world.* (The NASA Black Marble,
  playable.) +0 systems — a rendering rule, not a mechanic.
