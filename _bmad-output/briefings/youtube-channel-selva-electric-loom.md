## Art direction — one impossible mechanism, not spinning wallpaper

**Selected concept:** copper strands pass through jade combs, interlace into a legible tropical leaf-vein pattern, and curl beneath the mechanism to return as strands. A warm current follows the weave. It should feel like a precious instrument manufacturing tropical life from electricity.

- One hero sculptural loom; one beautifully composed fixed camera, medium-close enough to study the weave but wide enough to read input→pattern→return. No human/animal rig, city set, crowd or camera-tour workload.
- Blue-black surroundings/enamel, warm copper, deep jade and soft amber. Tactile finish: machined/polished edges against satin or translucent accents. Restrained bloom and attractive highlights, not neon clutter, chrome everywhere, or a stock mandala.
- Show genuine coordinated motion: strands visibly interlace, a clear vein/leaf motif emerges, the current travels through it, and the return keeps the process continuous. The mechanical payoff must be readable; a generic rotating ring or glow sliding over a static leaf is insufficient.
- Elegant, unhurried overall circulation with smaller interlocking rhythms. Maintain an attractive composition throughout, not one beauty pose surrounded by collisions. No visible strand/combs penetration, popping, teleporting, harsh flashes or reversing time to reset.
- Treat the leaf as a crafted geometric motif, not a simulated living plant. Constrained paths, periodic transforms, shape keys/geometry nodes and controlled instancing are appropriate. Avoid cloth/fluid/rigid-body simulation unless demonstrably indispensable; this is not an engineering-accurate industrial loom commission or a new simulation-framework project.
- Free/CC0 small materials/HDRI or existing reusable textures may help; do not undertake asset shopping or large downloads. A bespoke hero mechanism is appropriate under `docs/video-lane-asset-doctrine.md`; procedural materials are sufficient if they look good.
