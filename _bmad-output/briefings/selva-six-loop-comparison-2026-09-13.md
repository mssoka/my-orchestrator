## The five commissioned alternatives — substantive motion, not recolours

### 1. Breathing City — `breathing-city`
A compact sculptural street grid in blue-black ceramic/enamel. A warm copper current travels underneath and raises hinged architectural tiles into a fern-like canopy, then the tiles settle back into the recognizable grid in a continuous rolling breath. Lyric anchor: **“The concrete learned to breathe tonight.”** One kinetic object, NOT a city set or old narrative assets. Distinct silhouette and meaningful geometric transformation, not equalizer towers bouncing at different heights.

### 2. Copper Rain Garden — `copper-rain-garden`
Raindrop-like glass beads circulate through nested copper channels and jade leaf-shaped cups. Each bead's arrival tips/activates a cup and transfers it cleanly to the next; reflections and precise handoffs are the pleasure. Constrained authored motion is preferred to fluid/rigid-body simulation. Make the circulation/return physically readable and seam-continuous; avoid beads visibly teleporting, intersecting cups, unsupported drops or whole cycles of dead space. The wet-street/jungle/current association should survive without lyrics on screen.

### 3. Botanical Music Box — `botanical-music-box`
A beautiful rotating brass pin cylinder lifts emerald abstract frond/leaf keys in staggered patterns around warm central light. The viewer can follow the connection between driver and follower: pins arrive, keys lift/open, then return with deliberate easing. A sonidero rhythm expressed mechanically, not a generic rotating drum with stationary decoration. No new sound-effects/audio-production lane; the original song remains untouched.

### 4. Nocturnal Iris — `nocturnal-iris`
Nested copper and petrol-enamel shutter/petal rings counter-rotate around an amber light. The overlaps briefly resolve into a clean leaf-shaped opening, then separate into another intricate pattern. Aim for a dimensional jewel-like mechanism and attractive negative space, not a flat stock mandala or harsh strobing. Theme: nocturnal circular movement and paper-lantern warmth without an animal rig. Check all phases for panel penetration or an unexplained black/empty middle.

### 5. Canopy Press — `canopy-press`
Dark ceramic tiles pass through a sculptural brass press/roller. A clear press action reveals a jewel-green botanical relief; the output tiles form an undulating canopy and follow a continuous return beneath the mechanism. Anticipation→precise contact→transformed output is the satisfying action. Controlled relief/hinge/shape-key animation is sufficient; do not attempt a manufacturing/cloth simulation framework. The press must appear to act on the passing tile, not float through it or be decorative background.
