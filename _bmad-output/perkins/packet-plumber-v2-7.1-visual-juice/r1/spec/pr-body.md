## Renderer findings (recorded in the look-book §6 amendment)

1. The pre-7.1 primitive house's roof triangle was silently CULLED by the software
   rasterizer (winding order) — it never rendered in any T2 golden (also invisible in
   the GPU app? the fixed winding is now the fallback path).
2. rlsw's line primitives draw OPAQUE regardless of alpha — every translucent line
   draw diverges between the GPU app and the goldens. The 7.1 draws pre-composite
   instead of alpha-blending (hint lanes, congestion halo, crisis outline).

## Decisions & rationale

- **Sprite pipeline over vector primitives:** the user's r2 verdict ("looks like kids
  painting") settled it — Blender-designed sprites, committed PNGs + a committed
  generator + a bbox sidecar embedded via `#load` (regenerated sprites carry their own
  geometry; zero code churn). The render package loads them at init exactly like the
  HUD font (same files, app + harness, deterministic T2 pixels). Primitive draws stay
  as the belt-and-braces fallback (unreachable with committed assets).
- **Pre-composited translucency over alpha draws:** rlsw draws line primitives opaque
  (finding 2); alpha would make the app and the goldens disagree. Pre-composition is
  deterministic in both renderers and is what the blend *should* have looked like.
- **The game draws the contact shadow** (a flat translucent ink ellipse) rather than
  baking it into the sprites: Blender's hashed alpha dithers visibly at sprite size.
- **Lane-detail threshold (1.5× zoom):** the bird's-eye stays calm; the zoom reveals
  the full lane read — the progressive-disclosure camera model from the gate. The
  harness captures below the threshold, so the goldens pin the calm read.
- **Rejected:** baking shadows into sprites (dithered alpha); a free pan/zoom camera
  (the gate's model is auto focus-zoom; free camera can come later); removing lane
  colors at the bird's-eye entirely (3.3 canon keeps them — hinted, not deleted);
  Mini Motorways building shapes (the gate's "don't use the exact house images" — our
  own designs).
- **Bridges:** noted as an era-6 (SDN) candidate, out of scope for 7.1.
