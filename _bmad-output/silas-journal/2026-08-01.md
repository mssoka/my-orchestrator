## Lessons

- **Review-sensor baselines are in-memory** — a fresh Silas session has
  no catch-up for reviews posted while no Silas ran. The startup
  reconcile's direct `gh pr view` on every in-review PR covers the gap;
  keep that step non-negotiable.
- **Idle ≠ broken for in-review jobs.** Minion panes go idle the moment
  the PR opens; catch-up classification is only for panes stopped while
  the ledger says dispatched/clarifying/working.
