Hello! You want to make games. That is AWESOME. But here is a secret that grown-up builders know: great things are planned first and built second. Let me show you how we do it — with a little help from Gru and his minions.

🌟 Meet the Crew

🦹

Gru

The Boss With the Plan

Gru never builds anything himself. His job is to think first: What are we making? Who does what? He writes the plan, hands every minion a job card, and watches the big board. One brain plans, many hands build!

🍌

The Minions

The Builders

Each minion gets ONE job card (called a briefing) and builds exactly that. Minions are super fast and love bananas — but without a job card they build silly things. Like a door on the ceiling.

👨‍🔬

Dr. Nefario

The Watcher

Nefario invented a gadget (the nefario-watch) that pings Gru the moment a minion finishes, gets stuck, or has a question. Nobody has to guess — the gadget always tells.

🧐

Perkins

The Inspector

Perkins works at the Bank of Evil. Before any work is accepted, he checks it with SEVEN magnifying glasses (seven inspectors!). Something wrong? It goes back for fixing. All good? STAMP! Approved.

🧸

Bob

The Dreamer

Every few days Bob sleeps and dreams. While dreaming, he tidies everybody's notes and finds patterns — "three minions tripped on the same banana peel!" — so tomorrow, everyone is smarter.

👨‍👩‍👧

The Human

The Final Yes

The most important job of all! The robots help and the minions build, but a person always says the final yes before anything is really done. Helpers help. Humans decide.

🏰 Why Have a Boss? (The LEGO Castle Story)

Imagine 10 minions, and each one builds their own LEGO castle — with no plan. What do you get? 10 wobbly half-castles that don't fit together. One has no door. One is ALL door. One is upside down. 😵

Now imagine the same 10 minions with ONE plan, and Gru handing out jobs: "You build the towers! You build the walls! You — the dragon!" What do you get? ONE giant awesome castle — finished faster, with a dragon. 🐉

That is called orchestration — a big fancy word that means: one brain plans, many hands build. (Say it once and you sound very smart: or-ches-TRA-tion.)

🗺️ The Plan-First Machine

Before any minion touches a single brick, we run the Plan-First Machine (its grown-up name is BMad). It has steps:

1💭 Brainstorm — dream up LOTS of ideas. Silly ones too! Then pick the best.
⬇
2📋 The Brief — one page that says WHAT we are making and WHY.
⬇
3📐 The Blueprint — the drawings: what pieces exist and how they fit together.
⬇
4🗂️ Story Cards — tiny recipe cards. Each minion gets one, and it says EXACTLY what to make — and how to check it's right.
⬇
5🔨 NOW we build! — and Nefario watches, and Perkins checks, and the human says the final yes. 🎉

Skipping steps 1–4 is like baking a cake with no recipe. Sometimes you get cake. Usually you get... something else. 🫠 Talking to the AI is great — use it to plan first, and the building part goes ten times better.

🎮 P.S. Your Game Was Made This Way!

Your money game didn't appear by magic. Before ANY building started, there were days of planning games: five thinking games, 75 idea notes, a big decision board, and a coach named Carson asking tricky questions.

That's where the best ideas were born — the streets that never disappear, the secret money market, the tutor who explains words differently for every age. All of it was planned BEFORE anyone built anything. And that is why the pieces fit. 🧩
